How it works

From wallet to first trade in three steps.

i

Connect any Solana wallet

Phantom, Solflare, Backpack, Coinbase, Trust, Ledger and more, in your browser or on your phone. One sign-in message proves the wallet is yours. It moves nothing.

ii

Sign your limits

Pick Easy, Medium or Hard, then set the most per trade, a daily loss stop and how many trades may be open. You sign those exact words, and the robot cannot go beyond them.

iii

Fund, watch, withdraw

Send SOL to your ART vault, watch the robot in paper mode first, then go live when you are ready. Take your funds back to your wallet whenever you want.

Withdraw only to youThe vault can send funds to the wallet that signed in, and nowhere else.
Daily loss capThe robot stops for the day once your limit is reached.
Kill switchOne tap pauses the robot and closes every open trade.
Permissions

What you sign, and what it can do.

Connecting a wallet never gives anyone the power to spend from it. Only signatures do, and ART asks for exactly these.

iConnectShares your public address. Cannot move funds
iiSign inA message that proves you own the wallet. Moves nothing, grants nothing
iiiSign your limitsA message that sets the most the robot may risk. It lets ART trade from your vault only. Never from your own wallet
ivFund the vaultA normal transfer you approve in your wallet. The only time your wallet sends SOL
vWithdrawA message you sign. The vault pays your connected wallet and no other address. Always back to you
Be clear about custody. The vault is a wallet ART's trading service controls so it can trade while you are away. It is not a bank account and is not insured. Only fund what you can afford to lose.
